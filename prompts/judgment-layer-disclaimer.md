## What this audit does not do

PragmaWorks ensures everything before the **judgment layer** is correct. The judgment layer remains yours:

- **Domain expert review.** The AI knows the literature; it does not know the lived practice of a senior practitioner in your field. You either are that expert, or you bring one in.
- **Real user testing.** Synthetic users are not users. Real humans in their actual context, on their actual hardware, generate signal that no automated system replaces.
- **Compliance and legal sign-off.** A regulator, an auditor, or a lawyer reads the artifact and signs. The social and legal weight of the sign-off is constitutive of the act.
- **Aesthetic and strategic decisions.** Is this UI good? Does this story land? Should this feature exist at all? These are perception and judgment, not measurement.

GS does the 90% you couldn't get to anyway. You still own the irreducible 10%.

For the full conceptual treatment of the judgment layer, see the white paper at [doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19637142](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19637142).
